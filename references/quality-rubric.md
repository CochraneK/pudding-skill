# Quality rubric

Score each category from 0–2 before delivery. Target at least 16/20 and no zeroes.

| Category | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Question | unclear | implicit | explicit and answerable |
| Evidence | unsupported | partly checked | traceable to data/source |
| Insight | generic | descriptive | specific and meaningful |
| Narrative | disconnected | understandable | coherent progression |
| Visual choice | decorative | serviceable | directly serves task |
| Annotation | absent/confusing | adequate | concise and evidence-adjacent |
| Interaction | gratuitous/broken | works | meaningfully advances story |
| Mobile | broken | usable | intentionally designed |
| Accessibility | missing | partial | keyboard/reduced-motion/text equivalents |
| Attribution | misleading | incomplete | clear source + upstream attribution |

## Automatic rejection conditions

Do not deliver if any apply:

- quantitative copy cannot be reproduced from the supplied data;
- the page implies causality from correlation without evidence;
- essential information exists only on hover;
- the mobile chart is clipped or unreadable;
- a proprietary logo/font is hotlinked without permission;
- the project claims affiliation with The Pudding;
- the build fails.
